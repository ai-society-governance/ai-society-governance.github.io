## Getting there

The Panthéon is located in the Latin Quarter, within walking distance of **Luxembourg** (RER B) and **Cluny–La Sorbonne** (Métro 10).

**From Paris Charles de Gaulle Airport (CDG)**  
Take the RER B towards Paris and get off at **Luxembourg**. The journey takes approximately one hour.

**From Paris Orly Airport (ORY)**  
Take Métro line 14 towards Saint-Denis–Pleyel and connect to the Paris public transport network. Depending on the connection, the journey to the Panthéon takes approximately 40–50 minutes.

**From Gare du Nord**  
Take the RER B southbound and get off at **Luxembourg**. From there, the Panthéon is approximately a 10-minute walk.

**From Gare de l'Est**  
Take Métro line 4 towards Bagneux–Lucie Aubrac and get off at **Saint-Michel** or **Odéon**. The Panthéon is approximately a 10–15 minute walk away.

For detailed and up-to-date public transport information, please consult the [Île-de-France Mobilités journey planner](https://www.iledefrance-mobilites.fr/).
